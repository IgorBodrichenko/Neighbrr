Our Story

Why we built
Neighbrr.

It started on an ordinary weekday afternoon.

Driving through a neighborhood, bobbing and weaving through landscape trucks parked on both sides of the road - it felt like one at every house. Not because everyone found the best landscaper. But because nobody knew who their neighbors were using. Nobody had a way to share it.

"Imagine if I could simplify that."

That thought became Neighbrr.

Most homeowners have an endless list of things to accomplish around the house - landscaping, snow removal, a new roof, or something as simple as painting one room. Finding the time is stressful enough. Why add to that stress by scrolling through paid sponsors on Google, sifting through Angi listings, or posting in a local Facebook group hoping someone responds?

And if you just moved to a new area? You do not know a single contractor. You do not know who your neighbors trust. You are starting completely from scratch - cold searching strangers and hoping for the best.

The best contractors are not always the ones
with the biggest advertising budgets.
They are the ones your neighbor already used.

That knowledge has always existed - it just lived in text messages, backyard conversations, and neighborhood group chats that most people never see. Neighbrr puts it on a map.

Pull up your neighborhood, filter by the trade you need, and browse real verified contractors your actual neighbors have already used. No paid listings. No anonymous reviews. No cold searching. Just your neighbors - real people on your street - vouching for contractors they have already hired and verified.

Real contractors. Real homeowners. Real neighborhoods.

Trusted contractors who communicate and perform quality work are out there. We built the map to find them. We hope Neighbrr removes the stress of not knowing who to call - and makes finding a great contractor feel less like a gamble and more like asking the neighbor next door.

Now open that map, drop some pins,
and be the good Neighbrr everyone is looking for.
The Street Champion title is waiting. Go get it.
Do not just cold search ... brrrrr.
